### Berserk-inspired banner

Outputs: `public/berserk-day.png`, `public/berserk-night.png`, and matching `.webp` files.

Day prompt: Create a new extra-wide 3:1 anime landscape portfolio banner, using the former banner only as a reference for delicate ink-and-painted-field style. A peaceful Berserk-inspired moment of resolve: Guts, recognizable short spiky black hair, black cloak and armor, enormous broad rectangular Dragonslayer sword resting beside him, sits quietly in a sunlit meadow of pale wildflowers, gazing toward layered distant mountains. Small figure on the right third, generous open landscape and pale blue sky. Fine hand-inked details, soft airy blue, sage, ivory and warm daylight. Keep the subject and horizon in the central horizontal band for responsive crops. No helmets, gore, text, logos, or borders.

Night edit prompt: Edit the exact daylight illustration into a quiet moonlit version. Preserve the precise 3:1 framing, mountain silhouettes, flowers, seated pose, cloak and sword position as closely as possible. Change only illumination, palette and sky: deep navy-black, delicate stars, offscreen moonlight from the upper left, and silver-blue rim light on the armor, mountains and flowers. Keep detail readable at banner size. No horror, blood, red eclipse, text, or extra objects. The same landscape at night, not a new composition.
